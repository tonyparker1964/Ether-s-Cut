# Ether Cut

A browser-based music-video editor from Ether. Drop in videos, images and a song, arrange them on a timeline or storyboard, time scenes to the music, and export a finished video.

## Features
- Timeline and storyboard views
- Frame shapes: 21:9, 16:9, 9:16, 1:1
- Per-clip trim, length, motion (push, pull, pan, drift), framing, transitions and on-screen text
- Song waveform, tap-to-mark scenes to the beat, or split the song evenly
- Save and reopen storyboard plans (JSON)
- Export to MP4 or WebM, recorded in real time in the browser

### Animation and effects
- Keyframe animation on any clip or layer: scale, position, rotation and opacity, with easing and presets (zoom punch, slide in, spin in, float, impact shake and more)
- Layers track above the picture for overlays, logos and text, with blend modes (screen, add, multiply, overlay, soft light) and opacity
- GPU effects (WebGL): glow, light leak, vignette, film grain, color split, heat ripple, glitch, zoom blur, kaleidoscope, exposure, contrast, saturation, warmth
- One-click looks: Golden hour, Cosmic glow, Film, Dream, Glitch, Noir; plus a project-wide look for the whole video
- Particles: embers, gold sparks on beats, twinkling stars, floating dust, golden orbs, fireflies, snow
- Beat detection from your song, with Beat react to pulse glow, zoom and color on every beat
- Animated text: fade up, pop, typewriter, word by word, karaoke and glow pulse

## Run it
Open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge or a recent Safari. Everything runs locally; no files are uploaded.
